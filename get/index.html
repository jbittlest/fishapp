Charts that work offshore

NOAA charts and seafloor relief, with California’s artificial reefs and marine protected areas built in. Download an area while you have signal and it keeps working with none.

Tides, weather and water temperature

Tides and solunar periods, wind, swell and rain radar loops, and satellite sea-surface temperature to find the breaks.

An AI first mate

Ask about conditions, reefs, your spots and your catch log. It answers from your boat data, even with no signal.

Navigation and safety

Go-To and planned routes with ETA, track recording, an anchor-drag alarm and an SOS screen with your position.

Looking for a spot? Browse California marine protected areas and artificial reefs.

FishApp is a navigation aid, not a substitute for official NOAA charts, a depth sounder, a VHF radio or a proper lookout. Charts, depths, forecasts, tides, regulations and AI answers come from public sources and can be wrong, out of date or missing. Always cross-check anything you rely on and use your own judgement on the water.
