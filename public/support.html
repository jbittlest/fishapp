Support

Help with FishApp — offline marine charts, GPS navigation and fishing tools.

Not for navigation. FishApp is a navigation aid, not a substitute for official NOAA charts, a depth sounder, a VHF radio or a proper lookout. Charts, depths, forecasts, tides, regulations and AI answers come from public sources and can be wrong, out of date or missing. Always cross-check anything you rely on and use your own judgement on the water.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/jbittlest/fishapp/issues. Include your device, iOS version and what you were doing. Never post your API key or exact private spots.

Common questions

Does it work without signal? Yes. Download an area in More › Offline charts while you have internet; the charts and a 7-day forecast, tide and water-temperature pack for that area are stored on your phone. GPS works with no signal.

Why does the anchor watch need location in the background? So it can keep checking your position and sound the alarm with the screen locked. It only runs while a watch, a track recording or a route is active.

Is First Mate free? Yes. It answers from your boat data offline. With your permission it can use a free cloud AI when you have signal, or Claude if you add your own Anthropic API key.

Where is my data? On your device, unless you share it or allow cloud AI. See the privacy policy. Back up from Spots & tracks › Export backup.
